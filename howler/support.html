Support for Howler

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Howler lets rugby league fans call bunker reviews and captain's challenges as they happen, and see how the crowd answered.

Contact

Write to michaelsaibai@gmail.com and you will get a reply within a few days. Howler is made by one person, so it may not be the same day.

If you are reporting another fan's behaviour, please use Report in the app instead — it reaches the moderation queue straight away and does not put the details in an email.

Common problems

My sign-in code has not arrived

Check the spam or junk folder first — the code comes from an automated sender and often lands there. Make sure the address you typed is the one you can read. A code can only be sent again after five minutes, so the button stays disabled until then. If nothing arrives after two attempts, write to us with the address you are using.

I want to delete my account

Open Profile in the app and choose Delete account. Your email address, your display name, how you voted and the team you support all go, and cannot be recovered. Comments you posted stay, attributed to “Fan” rather than to you. If you own a group you must delete the group first, because deleting it takes the room and every message away from everyone else in it.

Someone is being abusive

Every comment has a Report action, and every fan can be blocked from your own Profile. Reporting a comment sends it to moderation; blocking a fan hides everything they write from you. Neither tells the other person.

I want to change my display name

You cannot. A display name is unique and permanent — it is what identifies you beside everything you have said, and letting it change would detach people from their own history. Choose carefully at sign-up.

How do groups work?

A group is a private room for a handful of fans. Whoever creates it decides who is invited and who stays. The owner cannot leave their own group; their way out is to delete it. You can turn group invitations off in your profile at any time.

Send us a message

You do not need an account, and you do not need to be signed in.
